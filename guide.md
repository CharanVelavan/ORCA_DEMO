# ORCA-NTN showcase guide

Team SolarSailor · https://orca-demo-blush.vercel.app

**Demo only. No AI models are connected.** For fresh results, clone and run
https://github.com/CharanVelavan/ORCA using its reproducibility guide.

1. Start with Learn: architecture, model/agent responsibilities, KB graph and narrated film.
2. Choose one of ten scenarios, read the detailed briefing and open its recording. S1 offers a
   mock capture and a separately labeled past fine-tuned-Qwen capture; both are playback here.
3. Use the global Play/Pause, event steps, slider and Final state controls. Playback stays shared
   across pages. The recorded clock is simulation time, not today's wall clock.
4. Console shows released observations and recorded evidence. Resize panels, zoom the fitted flow
   and use the evidence tabs. No approval, attack or model connection action runs here.
5. Policy & Gaps exposes cited verdicts, reporting clocks and candidate findings at the playhead.
6. Recovery shows what the recorded rollout and re-attack checks actually observed.
7. Audit verifies the original bundled chain in your browser and exports public evidence. A public
   export omits exercise-control entries and is not the original complete chain.

All network links, attacks, notifications and recovery actions are emulated. Capture approvals were
scripted. Planned Readiness dimension counts are project mappings, not official scores or proof of
observed completion. The source/checksum manifest is available at /data/provenance.json.

For fresh inference, the main repository supplies macOS/Linux and Windows quick-start scripts,
verified Qwen/RAG downloads, endpoint setup and evaluation instructions. The hosted site does not
measure model performance.
