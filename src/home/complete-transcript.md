**THE PROBLEM**

In Chennai, emergency calls, hospital telemetry and payments rely on connected networks. When satellite backhaul fails, ORCA asks: what happened, is recovery safe, and who needs to know?

**SECURE SELF-HEALING**

ORCA coordinates secure self-healing for non-terrestrial networks, connecting cyber detection, incident response and policy review. Everything shown is emulated. This walkthrough uses deterministic mock agents, without live Qwen inference.
