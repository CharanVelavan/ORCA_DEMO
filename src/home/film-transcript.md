# ORCA-NTN method film transcript

## 01 · 01 / THE NETWORK

In Chennai, homes, hospitals and emergency services depend on connected networks. ORCA explores how an emulated satellite backed network can protect these everyday services.

## 02 · 02 / THE INCIDENT

An attacker uses stolen credentials to reach the satellite operator’s ground management network. Terminals are disrupted. A backup path may also be unsafe, and service providers need to be told.

## 03 · 03 / DETECT & PREDICT

ORCA collects telemetry and prepares features. Model one detects anomalies. Model two forecasts failure in minutes. Model three distinguishes faults from attacks, or abstains. Model four allocates limited capacity.

## 04 · 04 / DECIDE & COORDINATE

Role agents coordinate around the evidence. Retrieval searches the knowledge base for relevant sources. A deterministic rule engine applies verified records and returns cited verdicts. The agents explain; the human remains accountable.

## 05 · 05 / GUARDED RECOVERY

Before a fix, ORCA checks the backup path and tests the plan in a clone. In assisted mode, a human approves the exact plan. The change rolls out in stages: five, twenty five, then one hundred percent. Re-attack checks and rollback guard the result.

## 06 · 06 / LEARN & IMPROVE

Services can recover while a hash chained audit preserves the evidence. The gap analyst records potential policy gaps for human review. Models detect, agents coordinate, code decides, and humans approve.
